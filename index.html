Spanish Immersion for WCSD

Families are asking the Walnut Creek School District to create a Spanish-English immersion program for our children.

Spanish immersion is not an extra class competing with math, science, reading or writing. Students learn core academic subjects through both English and Spanish.

More languages. More opportunity. A brighter future.

Prop 58 / CA Education Code § 310

Parents can start a formal district review

California law establishes a process for parents to request a new language acquisition program.

When enough families request a language program:

30

students at one school

or 20

students in the same grade

10 DAYS

The district gives written notice within 10 school days.

Notification of the request is issued to parents of pupils attending the school, the school's teachers and administrators, and the district's English learner advisory committee and parent advisory committee.

60 DAYS

The district makes its determination within 60 calendar days.

The district must determine whether it is possible to implement the requested language acquisition program and provide written notice of that determination to parents of pupils attending the school, the school's teachers, and administrators.
